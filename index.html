CHAPTER 04 · FROM RAGUL, WITH LOVE

A little letter for Gowtham.

Some things are easier to write than say out loud...

Dear Gowtham,

Who would've thought that stealing your ID card on our college-to-home bus journey would give me a best friend like you? 😂 What started as a funny moment became one of the most precious parts of my life.

You're not just a friend to me. You're my person, my partner in nonsense, and someone who makes ordinary days memorable. Thank you for every laugh, every tease, every conversation, and every moment we've shared.

Our daily conversations have become part of my day. Honestly, without talking to you, my day feels unfinished. We can talk for so long and still find something else to say. Thank you for being my comfort, my laughter, and my chosen family.

I know you are preparing hard for your government examination. I am praying that your hard work brings you success and that you get the government job you dream of. May you stay confident, focused, and blessed every step of the way. 🙏💙

No matter how busy life gets or where our paths take us, I hope we never lose this bond. We still have so many memories to make, so many places to go, and so many stupid things to laugh about.

From the ID card incident to a friendship that means everything — I'm glad it was you.

Always your best friend,
Ragul 🫂🖤
